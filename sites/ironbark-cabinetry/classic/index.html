How a project runs

Four steps, one point of contact

Marcus quotes every project personally and stays your contact from first sketch to handover.

  1. Consult

    A free chat in the studio or your home. We measure, talk budget honestly and sketch options on the spot.

    Free · 1–2 hours

  2. Design & quote

    Scaled drawings and a finishes board from Aiko, with a fixed, itemised quote. Nothing proceeds until you're happy.

    1–2 weeks

  3. Craft

    Your cabinetry is machined, assembled and finished on our Brunswick benches. You're welcome to visit mid-build.

    4–6 weeks

  4. Install & care

    The makers install it themselves, usually in a day or two, then return for a free adjustment visit after it settles.

    1–2 days on site

The studio

Fifteen years at the bench

I started Ironbark in 2011 with one bench, a borrowed thicknesser and a stubborn idea: cabinetry should be built like furniture, not flat-pack with better handles. Fifteen years on, we're still a three-person studio, and that's deliberate.

Being small means I quote every project myself, our designer Aiko draws it, and the same two sets of hands that machine your cabinetry carry it into your home. We use sustainably sourced Victorian ash and oak veneers, cut joints that will outlive the mortgage, and fit soft-close hardware as standard because slamming doors shouldn't be a thing you pay extra to avoid.

If you're within a few suburbs of Brunswick and want cabinetry that feels like it grew in the house, come and see the workshop. The kettle's always on.

— Marcus Reid, founder & cabinet maker
