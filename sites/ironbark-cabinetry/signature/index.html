No. 02

Measure twice, then measure again

Walk into the Brunswick workshop on a Tuesday and you will find three people, one wall of clamps, and the smell of sawdust and coffee. That is the whole company. Marcus draws, Tom machines, Aiko finishes — and all three install, because the person who cut the joint should be the one who stands it against your wall.

The person who cut the joint should be the one who stands it against your wall. Workshop rule no. 4

We build furniture-grade cabinetry: solid timber where it matters, premium board where it is smarter, dovetails you will never see and soft-close hardware you will use ten thousand times. Nothing leaves the workshop until it would survive a house move.

Every commission starts with a conversation and a tape measure, and ends with a piece that fits your walls to the millimetre — walls which, in this part of Melbourne, are never straight. That is not a problem. That is the job.
