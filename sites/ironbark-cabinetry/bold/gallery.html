Project gallery

Six projects. Six streets. One workshop.

Every piece below was designed, built and finished at 14 Foundry Lane, Brunswick, then installed by our own crew. Real client photography drops in here on the live site — these plates show the build intent.

  1. Walk-in wardrobe

    Fitzroy

    A spare bedroom in a Victorian terrace turned into a full dressing room — blackwood drawer towers, a double hanging bay and lit shelving that switches on as the door opens. Every panel was scribed on site to walls that hadn't been straight since 1902.

  2. Oak-veneer kitchen island

    Carlton North

    A 3.2-metre island in matched Grampians Ridge oak veneer, wrapped in a waterfall stone top with seating for four. Push-to-open bins and appliance drawers keep both working faces completely handle-free.

  3. Floor-to-ceiling library wall

    Northcote

    Five bays, 3.4 metres tall, holding a little over eleven hundred books with closed storage below and a rolling ladder on a brass-finish rail. The client's only brief: "make the television disappear" — it lives behind the centre doors.

  4. Floating bathroom vanity

    Brunswick East

    A wall-hung twin-drawer vanity in moisture-sealed timber with a honed stone top and a warm light strip beneath for midnight water runs. Internal drawers are dovetailed and finished inside and out — open them and they still look like furniture.

  5. Entertainment unit with hidden cabling

    Coburg

    A floating slatted unit with every cable routed through in-wall conduit we planned with the client's electrician before a sheet was cut. Amp, console and streamer sit behind acoustic slat doors — remotes work straight through them.

  6. Butler's pantry

    Princes Hill

    A working back-kitchen with open jar shelving, a stone landing bench for the morning coffee ritual and an appliance garage that swallows the toaster and mixer. A full-height sliding door in vertical-grain timber closes the whole thing away mid-dinner-party.
