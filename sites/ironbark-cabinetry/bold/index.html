Good questions

Asked at nearly every consult.

How long does a project take, start to finish?

Most projects run 8–12 weeks: two to three weeks of design and drawings, six to eight weeks on the bench, then one to two days on site to install. We confirm your install date in writing when you approve the drawings — and we hold it.

Is the quote really fixed?

Yes. After the site measure you get an itemised written quote, GST included, valid for 60 days. The only thing that changes the price is you changing the design — and we re-quote in writing before proceeding.

What materials and hardware do you use?

Carcasses in premium birch ply, doors and panels in Grampians Ridge oak veneers, Otway blackwood or two-pack polyurethane in any colour. Stilta soft-close hinges and runners are standard on every job — never an upsell.

Can you work with our architect or builder?

All the time. We price from architectural drawings, produce our own shop drawings for sign-off, and coordinate install timing with your builder so joinery arrives exactly when the site is ready for it.

What does the warranty cover?

Seven years on workmanship, in writing. Hinges, runners, alignment, finish defects — if it's our work, we fix it. Every project also gets a free adjustment visit after the joinery has settled into your home.
