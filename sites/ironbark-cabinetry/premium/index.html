N° 02 — Method

How a commission unfolds

Four unhurried stages, one maker responsible from first sketch to final hinge adjustment.

  1. I.

    Consult

    A complimentary design consultation at the studio or your home. We talk through the space, how you live in it, materials and budget — and we listen more than we talk.

  2. II.

    Design

    Measured drawings, veneer and finish samples you can hold, and a fixed, itemised quote. Nothing goes to the workshop until every detail is signed off.

  3. III.

    Craft

    Built in our Brunswick workshop by Marcus, Tom and Aiko. Hand-fitted joinery, dovetailed drawers where they earn their keep, and finishes rubbed back between coats.

  4. IV.

    Install

    Installation by the makers themselves, the room left clean, and a complimentary adjustment visit once the piece has settled in. Workmanship is guaranteed for seven years.

N° 05 — The studio

Built like furniture, because it is furniture

I started Ironbark in 2011 with a second-hand panel saw and a stubborn idea: cabinetry screwed to a wall deserves the same care as a dining table. Fifteen years on, that idea still runs the workshop.

There are three of us. Tom has been on the bench with me for nine years; Aiko runs design and finishing and has a better eye than both of us. We build in Victorian ash and oak veneers, cut our dovetails where they earn their keep, and spray two-pack in our own booth so nothing leaves here we haven’t rubbed back ourselves.

We take on a limited number of commissions each year — enough to do them properly, never so many that your project waits in a queue behind a shopping centre fit-out.

— Marcus Reid, founder & head maker
