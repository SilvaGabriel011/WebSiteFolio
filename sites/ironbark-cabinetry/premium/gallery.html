The portfolio · Six selected commissions

Pieces with a story in the grain.

Every commission below was designed, built and installed by our own hands, within twenty minutes of the Brunswick workshop. Real client photographs are shown at your consultation — these drawings are how each piece began.

01 Fitzroy

Walk-in wardrobe

A terrace bedroom with not one square corner became a full wall of open dressing storage — shelving, a brass rail and a bank of ash drawers, every edge scribed to the crooked plaster. Concealed lighting above the top shelf means no more phone-torch mornings.

Crown-cut ash · brass rail · ten weeks

02 Carlton North

Oak-veneer kitchen island

Three metres of quarter-cut oak veneer under a honed stone top, the grain matched so it runs unbroken from one waterfall end to the other. Drawers open on both faces, so breakfast and homework can share it without a border dispute.

Oak veneer · stone top · eight weeks

03 Northcote

Floor-to-ceiling library wall

Five bays from skirting to cornice, sized around the owners’ tallest art books and stiffened so fully loaded shelves will never sag. The aged-brass rolling ladder was the one indulgence they argued about — and now the thing they mention first.

Victorian ash · brass ladder · nine weeks

04 Brunswick East

Floating bathroom vanity

A wall-hung vanity in moisture-rated oak veneer keeps the whole floor visible, which makes a small bathroom feel twice its size. A ribbon of soft light underneath guides midnight visits without waking anyone properly.

Oak veneer · concealed lighting · six weeks

05 Coburg

Entertainment unit with hidden cabling

A low slatted unit that lets the amplifier breathe and the remote signals through, while every cable drops out of sight through a chase built into the carcass. The television hangs on a concealed batten, so the wall reads as timber and plaster — nothing else.

Oak slats · integrated cable chase · seven weeks

06 Princes Hill

Butler’s pantry

Behind a sliding oak door, open shelving keeps the everyday jars an arm’s reach from the kitchen, with charcoal shaker cabinets below for everything the benchtop shouldn’t see. The morning mess now has a room of its own — and the kitchen stays photograph-ready.

Oak & two-pack · sliding door · eight weeks
