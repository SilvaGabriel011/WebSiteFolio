How a job runs

Four steps, no loose ends

  1. Measure & consult

    Rob or Sam comes to site, measures properly and talks through timbers, profiles and budget. $120, credited when you proceed.

  2. Fixed written quote

    An itemised quote in plain English, GST included, valid for 30 days. You know the full number before we order a stick of timber.

  3. Crafted in Hamilton

    Your job is built in our own workshop by the Calloway team — not ordered in from a catalogue. Progress photos on request.

  4. Install & handover

    Licenced installation, site left broom-clean, and our workmanship guarantee in writing. We're not done until the door swings sweet.

The Calloway family

Same bench, second generation

Dad opened the Hamilton workshop in 1987 with a table saw, a van and a rule he drummed into us: measure it twice, own it forever. Nearly four decades later the machines are better but the rule hasn't moved.

These days it's us — Rob on the tools and running jobs, Jenny running the office and the schedule, and Sam, our apprentice-turned-tradesman, keeping the pair of us honest. When you ring Harbourline you get a Calloway, not a call centre.

We're joiners, not salespeople. If a repair will do the job, we'll tell you before we quote you a replacement.

— Rob & Jenny Calloway, Harbourline Joinery
