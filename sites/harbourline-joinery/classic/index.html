How we work

Four steps, no surprises

The same process Ray Calloway ran in 1987, with better machines and a booking system.

  1. Measure & consult

    We visit, measure properly and talk through timber, finish and budget. The $120 measure fee comes off the job when you go ahead.

  2. Written quote

    An itemised quote in plain English within three business days — materials, labour and GST — valid for 30 days.

  3. Made in Hamilton

    Your job is machined, assembled and finished in our own workshop. You're welcome to drop in and see it on the bench.

  4. Fit-off & handover

    Installed clean, adjusted on site and left swept. Every job is backed by our workmanship guarantee and the builders licence on the wall.

Our story

Two generations of Calloways

Dad opened the doors in 1987 with a bench saw, a borrowed ute and a rule he never broke: measure it yourself, make it yourself, stand behind it yourself. Jenny and I grew up sweeping shavings in that shed, and when we took over the day-to-day we didn't change the rule — just the machinery.

Most of our work still comes the old way: someone's neighbour had us hang a door, or their brother's cafe got its counter off our bench. We're a small crew on purpose. It means the person who measures your job is the person who machines it, and the person who machines it is the person who fits it.

If you want to see how your job's coming along, drop into the workshop — the kettle's usually on.

Rob Calloway For the Calloway family — Harbourline Joinery

The story so far

  1. 1987 Ray and Maureen Calloway open a two-man workshop behind the Hamilton rail yards.

    First job: six panel doors for a terrace on the hill. They still open sweet.

  2. 1996 First commercial fit-out — a pub counter that's still pouring.

    The workshop takes on its first spindle moulder and its first apprentice.

  3. 2005 Rob finishes his apprenticeship at the family bench.

    Jenny takes over the office and the quotes start going out typed instead of handwritten.

  4. 2014 The second generation takes over the day-to-day.

    New premises in Hamilton, same phone number, same rule about standing behind the work.

  5. Today A small crew, a full order book and the kettle always on.

    Doors, stairs, windows and fit-outs across Newcastle — booked online or over the counter.
