How a job runs

From measure to hand-over

  1. I

    Measure & consult

    We visit, measure and listen. You get honest advice on timber, finish and budget on the spot.

  2. II

    Drawings & fixed quote

    Scaled drawings and a written fixed-price quote, itemised down to the hinges. Nothing proceeds until you're happy.

  3. III

    Made in Hamilton

    Your job is cut, jointed and finished on our own benches — never brokered out — and you're welcome to visit it mid-build.

  4. IV

    Install & hand-over

    Fitted clean, adjusted to the millimetre and left swept. We walk you through care and warranty before we go.

The Calloway family

Two generations at the same bench

Dad opened the Hamilton workshop in 1987 with a table saw, a wall of hand planes and a simple rule: if you wouldn't hang it in your own home, it doesn't leave the shop. Nearly forty years on, the planes are still on the wall and the rule hasn't changed.

These days it's me on the bench, Jenny running the office and the schedule, and our son Sam — third generation in the making — serving his time beside me. We still source our hardwoods from Hunter mills we've dealt with for decades, and we still finish everything by hand.

Doors, windows, staircases, fit-outs. That's all we do, and it's why builders, heritage architects and half of Hamilton keep our number on the fridge.

Rob Calloway
