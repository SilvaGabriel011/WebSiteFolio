The portfolio

Six jobs, told properly

Every piece below was measured, made and fitted by the Calloway workshop in Hamilton. Real client photos drop straight into these frames — the artwork holds the place of each finished job.

I

Heritage front door restoration

Cooks Hill

An 1890s terrace door stripped back, re-jointed and rebuilt around its original arched fanlight. New fielded panels were milled from recycled Hunter hardwood and the brass furniture restored rather than replaced.

Timber recycled tallowwood · Finish hand-rubbed oil

II

Custom timber staircase

Merewether

A full flight of blackbutt treads under a continuous curved handrail, built on our benches and installed in two days. The slender balustrade keeps the hallway light while meeting code to the millimetre.

Timber blackbutt · Workshop build six weeks

III

Cafe fit-out joinery

Newcastle West

A fluted-front service counter, back shelving and window seating for a corner cafe, fitted across two weekends so trade never stopped. Every carcass was pre-built in Hamilton to keep time on site short.

Program two weekends on site · Front fluted spotted gum

IV

Double-hung window rebuild

The Junction

Six original double-hung sashes rebalanced with new cords, weights and parting beads, and rot spliced out of the sills. The glass and the street-facing profiles are the ones the house was born with.

Scope six windows · Heritage glass retained

V

Boardroom wall panelling

Honeysuckle

Full-height fielded panelling and a picture rail for a harbourside boardroom, wrapped neatly around services and acoustic backing. Set out so every joint lands dead centre on the wall it faces.

Panels 32 fielded · Install after-hours, one week

VI

Solid timber bar counter

Hamilton

A ten-seat bar counter in solid spotted gum with a panelled front and a brass foot rail, five minutes' walk from our own workshop. The top was glued up as a single slab and finished by hand on the bench.

Top solid spotted gum · Rail aged brass
