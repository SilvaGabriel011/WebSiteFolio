How it works

From first cuppa to final drawer, in four steps

  1. Design consultation

    Dana or Priya visits your home, measures up and listens to how your household actually uses the kitchen. You'll get honest advice, not a sales pitch.

    $190 — credited in full when you book

  2. Fixed-price quote & plans

    Within a week you receive colour drawings, a finishes schedule and one fixed price, itemised line by line. Take your time — the quote holds for 60 days.

    No deposit until you approve the plans

  3. Build & install

    Our own crew, led by foreman Marco, runs the job — cabinetry, stone, tiling, plumbing and electrical co-ordinated for you. Tidy site, daily updates, no surprises.

    Refresh 5–7 days · Full Reno 3–4 weeks

  4. Handover & care visit

    We walk you through your new kitchen, hand over the care guide, then come back at six months to adjust hinges and check every drawer — free, always.

    Free 6-month care visit included

Family-run since 2011

The two of us, one crew, and 260-odd kitchens

Sunline started in 2011 with one ute, a garage full of door samples and a simple idea: kitchen renovations shouldn't be a gamble. Fifteen years on, we're still a small outfit on purpose — two designers, one crew, and a workshop ten minutes from the beach.

We're renovators, not order-takers. We'll tell you when a layout won't work, when a cheaper finish will look just as good, and when it's worth spending more. Because we design and build with our own team, the person who sketches your kitchen is the same person standing in it at handover.

And because we live here too — school runs, Saturday markets, the lot — our reputation walks around the same suburbs you do. That's why every quote is fixed, every site is left tidy, and every kitchen gets a care visit at six months.

Dana Whitfield & Priya Sharma Owners, Sunline Kitchens
