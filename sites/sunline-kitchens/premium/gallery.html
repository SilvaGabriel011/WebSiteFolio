The portfolio

Six kitchens, six households

Every project here started with a cuppa at someone’s bench and ended on a fixed price, delivered to the day. Illustrated plates stand in for client photography in this demonstration.

No. 01

The coastal white kitchen

Palm Beach

A dark 1980s galley opened to the deck and flooded with morning light, in crisp shaker whites over a soft sage island. The stone was chosen to read like wet sand — calm from a distance, full of movement up close.

Package Full Reno Benchtop Corella Stone ‘Shorebreak’ On site 13 days

No. 02

The Hamptons kitchen

Currumbin

Glass-front uppers, beadboard panelling and brass cup pulls for a family who host every second Sunday. The island seats five for homework and eleven for Christmas, and the good crockery finally lives on display.

Package Luxe Cabinetry Ashgrove Panel Co. shaker profile On site 15 days

No. 03

The minimalist oak kitchen

Miami

One timber, one stone, no handles — a pared-back kitchen for an architect couple who wanted the room to disappear when it isn’t working. Routed finger pulls and a single 3.6-metre shelf carry the whole composition.

Package Full Reno Fronts Riverline oak veneer, clear matt On site 11 days

No. 04

The stone & brass kitchen

Mermaid Beach

A moody, hotel-bar kitchen for a couple who entertain after dark — charcoal-green cabinetry, a monolithic veined island and one long brass bar light on a dimmer. The under-cabinet glow was tuned over three site visits until it was exactly right.

Package Luxe Benchtop Corella Stone ‘Midnight Vein’, waterfall ends On site 15 days

No. 05

The compact apartment kitchen

Burleigh Heads

Seven square metres, two home offices and a serious coffee habit — solved with a full-height pantry wall, drawers where cupboards used to argue, and a fold-down ledge that turns the balcony rail into a breakfast bar. Small kitchen, zero compromises.

Package Refresh Hardware Copperline soft-close throughout On site 8 days

No. 06

The indoor–outdoor entertainer

Tallebudgera

One continuous stone bench that starts at the cooktop and ends past the bifolds, over the deck. Inside it’s a working kitchen; outside it’s a servery, a bar and the best seat in the valley when the afternoon sun drops behind the palms.

Package Luxe Bench 6.4m continuous Corella Stone span On site 15 days
