How it works

Four steps, zero chaos

  1. Design

    An in-home consultation, then a concept you can walk through in 3D. We refine until it feels like yours — layout, storage, light, the lot.

  2. Selections

    One morning in our Burleigh studio choosing stone, colour and hardware from curated boards. Your fixed price is locked in writing that week.

  3. Build

    Cabinetry is made while your kitchen stays in service. On site, our own crew runs 10–15 working days — scheduled to the day, tidied every evening.

  4. Handover & care

    A walkthrough with the designers, a care kit for your finishes, then a 6-month visit to re-tune hinges and runners. All included.

The studio

Two designers. One team. No middlemen.

We started Sunline in 2015 because we kept watching beautiful designs die between the showroom and the site.

So we built a studio where that hand-off doesn’t exist. Dana designs, Priya runs the build, and the same crew — led by Marco, our site lead of seven years — is in your home from demolition to handover. When you call, you speak to one of us. When something needs deciding, it’s decided by someone who has stood in your kitchen.

We take on around twenty renovations a year across the southern Gold Coast. Enough to keep our trades ours; few enough that yours never queues behind someone else’s.

— Dana Whitfield & Priya Sharma, owners
