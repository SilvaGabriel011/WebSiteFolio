Jobs we're proud of.

Six recent projects from around the Penrith area — from midnight emergencies to full renovation pipework. Real job types, real suburbs, real turnaround times.

Jordan Springs

Bathroom renovation rough-in

Complete rough-in for a family ensuite renovation — new supply drops, shower and bath wastes, all set out to the builder's drawings. Passed council inspection on the first visit, keeping the reno on schedule.

Emu Plains

315L hot water system swap

The old cylinder let go overnight and the family rang at 7am — by mid-afternoon a new 315L electric unit was plumbed in with new valves and the old tank carted away. One fixed price, quoted over the phone and honoured to the dollar.

South Penrith

Strata backflow compliance

Annual testing of backflow prevention devices across a 24-unit strata complex, with every device tagged, tested and reported in a single morning. The strata manager gets the compliance paperwork lodged before the due date, every year.

Cranebrook

After-hours burst pipe repair

An 11:40pm call about water pouring through the garage ceiling — we had the main isolated by 12:20am and the split section cut out and re-piped before sunrise. The homeowner kept their carpet, and their insurer got tidy photos of everything.

Penrith

Full kitchen re-plumb

Complete re-plumb for a kitchen renovation — relocated sink supply and waste, new isolation valves, dishwasher connection and a certified gas point for the new cooktop. Coordinated around the cabinet installer so the job never stalled.

Mulgoa

Rainwater tank & pump hookup

Plumbed a new rainwater tank into an acreage property's garden taps and laundry, with an automatic pressure pump and mains changeover valve. The owners now run irrigation off free water all summer without lifting a finger.
