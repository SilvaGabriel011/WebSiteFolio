Selected work — 2025/26

Six jobs that show how we work

Every project here is real work from our patch, retold honestly — what the problem was, what we did, and how it was left. For a real client site, these illustrations swap out for photographs of the finished jobs.

No. 01Jordan Springs

Bathroom renovation rough-in

A full ensuite strip-out re-plumbed to a new layout — relocated waste, a recessed cistern and a floor-level shower channel set to millimetre falls. Every joint was pressure-tested and photographed before the wall sheeting went on, so the builder had proof, not promises.

Renovation·4 days on site·Certified & documented

No. 02Emu Plains

315L hot water system swap

The old cylinder let go at breakfast; by mid-afternoon a new 315L electric unit was plumbed, earthed and heating. The old tank left on the same trailer, and the valve service dates went straight into the owner’s reminder calendar.

Same-day·Supply & install·Old unit removed

No. 03South Penrith

Strata backflow compliance

Annual testing and reporting across a 24-unit strata complex, with two ageing devices replaced during the same visit to save a second call-out. Reports lodged ahead of the compliance deadline — three years running now.

Strata·Annual programme·Reports lodged on time

No. 04Cranebrook

After-hours burst pipe repair

A 2am call about water through a kitchen ceiling — mains isolated within the hour, the split section cut out and re-jointed by four. No call-out fee on the invoice, because we did the repair; that’s the promise working as intended.

Emergency·On site in 40 min·$0 call-out applied

No. 05Penrith

Full kitchen re-plumb

A 1970s kitchen re-plumbed for a new island layout — fresh hot, cold and waste runs, plus a gas line to the island cooktop with compliance certificate issued on handover. Pressure-tested, certified and finished in two days flat.

Renovation·2 days·Gas certified

No. 06Mulgoa

Rainwater tank & pump hookup

A 22,000-litre tank plumbed to the laundry, toilets and garden taps with an automatic mains switch-over for dry spells. First-flush diverter, pump housing and a level gauge included — the drinking water supply left untouched.

Acreage·Pump & switch-over·Garden & laundry fed
