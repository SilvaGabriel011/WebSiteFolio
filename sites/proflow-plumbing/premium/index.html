About ProFlow

One van in 2012. Three of us now — on purpose.

I started ProFlow with one van and a simple rule: do the job the way I’d want it done at my mum’s place. Fourteen years on, that rule hasn’t changed — the vans just got tidier.

There are three of us: myself, Mitch on emergencies and drains, and Lena, who runs our gas and compliance work. We’ve stayed small deliberately, because the person who quotes your job should be the person who turns up to do it.

We’re based in Penrith and we don’t pretend to service half of NSW. Western Sydney is our patch — which is why we can promise the hour, not the day.
